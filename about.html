About Fabrica

An independent guide written for people who want their clothes to last.

Fabrica started with a simple frustration: it is easy to find out what a garment costs and almost impossible to find out what it is. Composition labels are printed small, care symbols are not taught anywhere and size charts contradict each other from shop to shop.

What we publish

Two long guides, three free tools and nothing else. The Fabric Guide covers the fibres you meet on a label. The Fit & Sizing Guide covers measuring, ease and alterations. The tools on the home page answer the three questions we were asked most often while writing them.

How we stay independent

We do not take payment, gifts or samples in exchange for mentioning a brand, and you will not find shopping links here. Where we name a fibre or a technique, it is because it is useful, not because somebody paid for it. If ads appear on the site, they are served by a third party and have no influence on what we write.

How we work

Advice here is built from textile reference material, published care standards and ordinary wardrobe testing. Where something depends on the brand, the weave or the finish, we say so rather than inventing a rule. Our Editorial Policy sets out how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Tell us when we are wrong

If a tool gives you an odd answer or a guide contradicts your own experience, we want to know. Write to hello@fabrica.com or use the contact page, and we will look at it properly.
